Rules & training notes

Dealer stands on soft 17, peeks for blackjack, and blackjack pays 3:2. Double on any first two cards, including after splits. Split to four hands. Split aces receive one card each, with no resplitting. Split 21 pays 1:1. No surrender or insurance.

Hints are excluded from unassisted strategy accuracy. Shuffling keeps your results and bankroll; an unfinished round is abandoned without a payout. No automatic shuffle: with fewer than 16 cards, shuffle before dealing; if a round exhausts the shoe, that round is void.

The six-deck Hi-Lo bet ramp uses the minimum at true count +1 or lower, then 2×, 4×, 8×, 10×, and 12× the minimum at +2 through +6, capped by the table maximum. This is training guidance, not a promise of profit.

References: single deck, double deck, multi-deck strategy, Hi-Lo 1–12 bet ramp.
