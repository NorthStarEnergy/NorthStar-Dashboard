14-day water volume diagnostic

Mean-inflow errors in m³/s and total-volume errors in million m³. Only complete 14-day windows are counted. These use reconstructed weather and are separate from prospective scores.

Daily inflow diagnostic

Recovered 2020–2024 records train the long-history targets; 2025–2026 records test them. Kap and Nipissing have shorter histories: 2025 training and 2026 testing. Inputs use reconstructed rainfall, not archived issued forecasts. Errors are for daily inflow, and overlapping test origins are correlated. These results guide pilot selection; live forecast performance remains unproven.

Data & sources

Original workbooks remain read-only. Updated EAST/WEST files are checked on refresh. The pilot stores its own records and forecast archive.

Connected to NorthStar

Water Log inflows, upstream releases and elevations are read automatically. Kap uses the existing WSC collector. Rainy, Namakan and Lake of the Woods use public LWCB balances. Updated EAST/WEST workbooks are checked automatically.

Collection is checked every five minutes; weather is refreshed every six hours. Forecasts update when usable daily records change. A missing input stays visible rather than becoming zero.

Source distinctions

Workbook records, LWCB provisional balances, historical reanalysis and issued weather forecasts remain separately identified. Wetness is a relative model proxy, not a field measurement of saturation.

Snow or freezing conditions disable the uncalibrated rainfall increment. Current forecasts are research outputs, not operating instructions.
