Infrastructure and settings

The web server, authentication, database and tunnel stay available so you can return here and restart stopped services. They are not process-kill controls. Credentials and operating-system tasks remain in their existing administrative tools.

Water providers have separate collection intervals, lookback windows and station selections. Daily reports have their own daily schedule. Open their settings from the service card; both the master service and its individual schedule must be enabled.

The Codex inflow monitoring reminder is paused separately. It does not run inside NorthStar.
