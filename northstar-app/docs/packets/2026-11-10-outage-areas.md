# Outage register areas

Click **Area** on the register to sort by the primary site's operating area. Clicking again reverses the order. Use **Advanced settings → Area** to find outages affecting any site in the selected group. Each outage appears once, with its primary area first. Unassigned sites remain visible and sort last in either direction.

| Area | Sites |
| --- | --- |
| West | Kenora, Norman, Calm Lake, Fort Frances and Sturgeon Falls |
| KAP / OKI | Kapuskasing group (Big Beaver Falls, Camp Three Rapids, Old Woman Falls and White Otter Rapids) and Okikendawt |
| IR / TW / IS | Iroquois Falls, Twin Falls and Island Falls |
| Quebec | Stations in the NorthStar Quebec directory |

Fort Frances TS and Iroquois Falls 230 kV follow their respective station groups. The register derives areas from existing site names, station IDs and recognized abbreviations. Filtering and sorting apply before pagination across upcoming, active and past outages.
