# UWPC library reader

Manual, Forms & Tags and Practice Questions share a single toolbar with search and the protected source PDF link. Search applies to the selected section. The manual adds reading format, page navigation and a full-screen control. Narrow screens can scroll the toolbar horizontally.

The manual contents menu follows the width of its section titles, with long titles wrapping on smaller screens. Its edge button hides or restores the menu. Full screen expands the reader and keeps its page controls available. Resizing, collapsing and entering or leaving full screen preserve the current PDF frame.

The update uses static assets and takes effect on refresh.
